---
title: "Working with Claude: examples"
tags: [claude, examples, workflow, getting-started]
---
You don't call the tools yourself: you tell Claude what you want, in plain words, and Claude
follows the workflows of `CLAUDE.md`. The examples below are made up, to show the kind of request
that works and what Claude does with it.

Good requests say **what** to add or find, and leave the **where** to Claude: it finds the right
place from the tags and the existing nodes, and tells you what it did. If you care where a piece
of content goes, say so.
