This tree is the sample content of the project. It shows what a knowledge tree looks like, and it
explains what the rules in `CLAUDE.md` don't: how the tree works inside, what working with
Claude looks like, the MCP tooling in practice, and a methodology for specifying work with an
agent.

## Browsing it

- **The tree** on the left: click a title to open a node, the arrow to unfold it.
- **Search**: by tags (comma-separated, e.g. `sqlite, mcp`), or full-text on titles and content.
- **A node** shows its path, its tags, its content, its children and its links. Links go both
  ways: "links out" are the ones this node makes, "links in" are the ones other nodes make to it.
- **The size meter** under the title shows how much of the 8192-character limit the content uses.
- **Refresh** picks up what Claude changed since the page was loaded.

## What to do with it

Keep it, change it or build around it: it is ordinary content, and Claude edits it like any other.
The rules and workflows Claude follows are in `CLAUDE.md`, not here.
